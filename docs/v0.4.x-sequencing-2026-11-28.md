No amendment recorded the pause. The [v0.4.0 plan](v0.4.0-plan.md) held its record from [#1139](https://github.com/mkhomutov/Persatrix/pull/1139) on, and that record moves here whole now that the pause is over:

- The maintainer paused v0.4.0 on 2026-11-07 until the next strategy review, to be held after EXP-001's result or by 2026-12-15, where the maintainer would end it.
- Every [Progress](v0.4.0-plan.md#progress) row not ✅ waited, with the issue fixes it carries. A2, A4, B1 and C1 stopped part-way.
- Allowed meanwhile: EXP-001's own work and, by a ruling of 2026-11-21, the quality-review fixes (ISSUE-0196 onward) and dependency and CI upkeep.
- By the same ruling, [ISSUE-0196](issues/ISSUE-0196-shipped-shell-allowlist-voids-the-file-and-network-rules.md)'s environment half and [ISSUE-0213](issues/ISSUE-0213-closed-interaction-summaries-open-to-any-account.md) waited with row A4. The licence PR, the strategy and README rewrites and EXP-002's pre-registration waited for the review: [next steps](v0.4.x-sequencing-2026-10-21.md#next-steps) 2–5 of the Amendment 2026-10-21.
- [#1087](https://github.com/mkhomutov/Persatrix/pull/1087) to [#1090](https://github.com/mkhomutov/Persatrix/pull/1090) merged between 2026-11-07 and 2026-11-10, after the pause was decided, because the plan did not yet say so.
- The review of 2026-11-28 ruled that the pause lasts until this amendment merges.

### Why this changes

1. **The review has ruled, and only an amendment ratifies it.** On 2026-11-28 the maintainer accepted six recommendations as they were put. None binds until an amendment acts on it, and by the second the pause itself lasts until then.
2. **EXP-001 reported, and its rule does not decide the society work.** No rule fires as registered, since two AI raters scored in the two people's place. On the AI-scored figures rule 1 fires: arm C, the governed discussion, does not clearly beat arm A, one model call. Rulings (h) and (l) say what a fired rule decides, and whether the society work goes on is not part of it, so that was the review's to decide. Rule 1 is about one short job and cannot speak to a long task, so it is no reason to stop. It is also the only measurement so far, and it went against the governed discussion.
3. **Starting more rows before the next experiment is written would repeat finding F6**, that the core claim was [untested](project-strategy-four-reviews.md#1-what-the-four-reviews-established). EXP-002 is the experiment that can count against a society on a long task, and it has no pre-registration yet. The four part-way rows differ: stopping them leaves half-built work on `main`, and A4 closes [ISSUE-0152](issues/ISSUE-0152-one-quarantine-refuses-every-anonymous-caller.md), rated high.
4. **Ruling (t) passed three items to this review**, and it answered each. One, the governance-core extraction, has now been put to two reviews that ordered none. Nobody outside has asked for it, and EXP-001 gives no sign that the governed discussion is worth reaching for by itself.
5. **Four decisions made between amendments sat outside this record.** A ruling holds until an amendment says otherwise (rule 5):
   - ruling (e) still says the size check warns at 500 lines, and the checker has warned over 700 since [#1146](https://github.com/mkhomutov/Persatrix/pull/1146);
   - 57 issues carry severities that their filings proposed and nobody ruled;
   - the decision of 2026-11-16 on [ISSUE-0275](issues/ISSUE-0275-old-messages-from-a-loose-id-still-reach-agents.md) lives in an issue note and the channels guide, and release notes are drafted from Known Gaps;
   - the `Python 3.12 (unit tests)` job was left unrequired because it had no history on `main`. From 2026-11-21 to 2026-11-28 it ran 64 times, 18 of them on `main`, and passed every time, as the Python 3.11 job did on the same runs.
6. **The trust score waited for an experiment that could not answer.** The first review left it to EXP-001, and no EXP-001 arm varies trust.
