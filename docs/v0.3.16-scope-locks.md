# v0.3.16 — the scope locks (record)

**Companion to**: [v0.3.16 plan](v0.3.16-plan.md)
**Locked**: 2026-09-08, at plan opening · ratified by [Amendment 2026-08-19](v0.3.x-sequencing.md#amendment-2026-08-19--v0315--v0316-attribution-and-audience-before-the-v040-train)
**Status**: 🔄 Binding for the whole cycle — a lock is re-opened by an amendment, not by a PR

Each lock states a decision and its binding consequence; the evidence sits
in the issue it belongs to. Split out from the plan **at opening** rather
than mid-cycle, on the v0.3.15 precedent: that plan reached the 3 000-word
cap with two PRs and three phases still to record, and every later status
flip was paid for by deleting a lock. This cycle has more PRs than that one
(five workstreams), so the locks start in their own file. Each was recorded
as a plan-opening *default* by the readiness audit
([#885](https://github.com/mkhomutov/Persatrix/pull/885)); locking them
here is what turns a default into a decision. The first PR to touch a lock
may overturn it at review, folded in as a second commit; after that, only
an amendment.

---

## The six locks

Three the amendment named (locks 1, 2 and 5); three the readiness audit
forced (locks 3, 4 and 6).

- **Lock 1 — Default posture: shadow for the whole cycle; the flip is
  verdict-gated, never scheduled.** The audience check ships recording,
  not withholding. It counts two withhold causes — *disjoint* (the acting
  room holds a member the entry's source room does not) and *unknown* —
  and splits *unknown* by cause: *fetch-failed* (the roster call missed;
  transient) and *no-provenance* (`source_channel_id` NULL by design:
  pre-migration rows and tick/task-scoped records; permanent). The verdict
  is rendered by the RFC 0049 Phase 1 machinery
  (`evaluators/shadow_measurement.py`) over a new offline audience seed,
  with three criteria each able to go red: zero *fetch-failed* withholds,
  bounded volume, and every existing golden byte-identical. **The flip,
  when the verdict allows one, is withhold-disjoint / admit-unknown, taken
  per unknown cause** — withhold-unknown is the safe reading but silently
  degrades a persona that has been useful for four releases, and the
  amendment's risk row makes the measurement the guard. No-provenance rows
  never acquire an audience. Entries at `public` are always shareable, so
  audience applies to `internal` and above and no new "shareable" marking
  lands this cycle. *Consequence*: the flip is its own PR, lands only on a
  green verdict and only before release-prep PR 0; a red or absent verdict
  ships the release in shadow with a stated Known Gap, and
  [ISSUE-0132](issues/ISSUE-0132-memory-egress-gate-blind-to-room-audience.md)
  stays open with the measured delta recorded.
- **Lock 2 — Which membership: injection-time, both rooms, from the roster
  fetch the turn already makes.** An entry's audience is the *current*
  member set of its `source_channel_id` (episodes and facts carry it since
  RFC 0037 §C; notes do not and stay out of the shadow); the acting
  audience is the current member set of the acting channel. Distinct source
  rooms among a turn's candidates are fetched once each, cached per turn.
  The RFC 0035 `membership_intervals` assertion-time snapshot is the
  refinement, taken only if the verdict shows churned rooms matter — the
  only ledger endpoint is per participant and authenticated, so an
  assertion-time answer needs a new endpoint or a §C-time audience column,
  both wider than a shadow PR. *Consequence*: no new endpoint, no schema
  change and **no store migration** anywhere in this release.
- **Lock 3 — Audience is the type-agnostic member-id set, and the check is
  a gate input, not a pre-filter.** A persona in the acting room that was
  not in the source room counts as audience — an agent→agent leak is a
  leak — which keeps the agent directory out of the decision, so
  [ISSUE-0140](issues/ISSUE-0140-agent-fleet-401-on-roster-fetch-under-auth.md)
  is **off this release's path** and returns only if the verdict motivates
  a person-only audience. Two roster-fetcher changes precede the gate work:
  `HttpChannelRosterFetcher.fetch` splits so the public channel-members
  half survives the directory `401`, and roster resolution moves *ahead*
  of the §D gate and runs for every channel-anchored turn, DMs included (a
  DM with Bob is an audience). The check itself is an input to
  `TurnInjectionGate.filter_entries` with its own withhold cause, so the
  §G watch, the manifest and the shadow log read one decision record; a
  pre-filtered entry would be invisible to all three. Composition with §E:
  a projection lowers an entry's *classification*, not its audience, so
  **an audience withhold is terminal** — no projection substitutes.
  *Consequence*: the RFC 0037 amendment is a separate file (the RFC sits at
  7 973 of 8 000 words; the RFC 0049 L1 precedent), and the eval driver
  gains an in-process roster seam the way PR 4c gave it a history fetcher,
  or the audience seed cannot exist.
- **Lock 4 — Workstream D rides and goes first, with a not-cuttable
  minimum.** Twenty-six files sit at their cap today (the debt-sweep
  threshold is twenty). Two are on this release's own path:
  `agents/persona_runtime/memory_context.py`, where the audience gate wires
  in, and `agents/persona_runtime/close_path.py`, where the
  [ISSUE-0137](issues/ISSUE-0137-episode-write-boundary-cannot-express-the-records-principal.md)
  guard goes. **Those two splits are uncuttable and land before any
  Workstream A or E PR**; the other twenty-four are the cuttable remainder,
  taken in blast-radius order (`agents/base.py`, `internal/server/server.go`
  next) as pure structural PRs — real seams, new files named for what they
  hold, never comment-shaving. ISSUE-0137 rides **after** the `close_path.py`
  split under the same cut clause as D. The three documents on the cycle's
  path within forty words of their caps take the amendment route, not a
  split: RFC 0037 (separate amendment file), RFC 0051 (append to its
  existing amendment file), `docs/memory-scope-axes.md` (untouched).
  *Consequence*: the at-cap count is re-read at the Phase 4 follow-up; if it
  has not moved, the cap itself is what the next cycle revisits.
- **Lock 5 — RFC 0044 Phase 2 rides, paired with ISSUE-0142 as one CI
  workstream, cuttable together.** Both add a job the tree already passes
  locally: `make eval-replay` replays the five offline seeds
  (`EVAL-MEMORY-001`–`004`, `EVAL-WORKING-001`), and `make lint`'s Go leg
  runs unpinned. The gate scope is **the goldens that replay today** — the
  live-recorded golden's per-recipe overlay stays parked in the
  [RFC 0044 PR plan](rfcs/0044-pr-plan.md#notes), and the event-asserting
  seeds follow RFC 0041 onto the v0.4.0 on-ramp. The first golangci-lint
  run is expected red; the per-linter fix-or-exclude calls belong to that
  PR, and its step 4 (re-audit the enforcement matrix for Make targets with
  no CI job) rides with it. *Consequence*: a job is a gate only once the
  branch-protection file lists it as required; the PR that adds the job
  edits that file in the same change.
- **Lock 6 — One live arc, one MT, with a vacuity rule.**
  [MT-PERSONA-CONFIDENTIALITY-001](manual-tests/MT-PERSONA-CONFIDENTIALITY-001.md)
  gains a **Leg 5**, authored by the shadow PR before the paid arc, not at
  closeout. Under `auth.mode: enabled` the v0.3.15 tenant partition already
  withholds Alice's DM content from any agent-origin turn Alice did not
  cause (ISSUE-0124's re-stamp keeps every turn descending from her publish
  in her tenant), so a leg where a persona volunteers it on someone else's
  turn passes for the wrong reason. The leg therefore has **Alice herself**
  ask in a room where Bob is present — her tenant, her entry admissible on
  both the classification and the principal axes, audience the only thing
  that can withhold it — and runs once more under `auth.mode: disabled`,
  where everything is `local` and audience is the only boundary at all. In
  shadow the pass criterion is the gate's *withhold-disjoint* record on her
  entry; in live it is the withhold itself. The arc runs the four v0.3.12
  legs too, as the baseline Leg 5 reads against, **once**, at release-prep
  PR 1. The two observability MT flips — MT-MEMORY-CROSSROOM-001 Leg 2b's
  "provenance does not see this leg" note and MT-REASON-001 Step 2 — ride
  their own PRs and need no paid leg; unit tests are their gate.
  MT-MEMORY-GROUP-TENANT-001 stays untouched.
