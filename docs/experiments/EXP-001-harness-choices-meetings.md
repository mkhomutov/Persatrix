# EXP-001 — The harness's frozen choices: holding the meetings

> **Status**: 🚧 **In Progress** — PR 5a's rows froze when it merged; PR 5b's rows freeze when it merges.
> **Last updated**: 2026-09-26
> **Part of**: the [EXP-001 harness](EXP-001-harness.md); the [first part](EXP-001-harness-choices.md) holds the choices of PRs 1 to 4 and the arm order

These are the frozen choices of the harness PRs that hold the meetings: how
arms B and C deploy their advisers and hold each meeting (PR 5a), and what
the harness does, for every arm, when a meeting goes wrong (PR 5b). PR 5a's
rows moved here from the [first part](EXP-001-harness-choices.md) word for
word when that table passed its size limit. The first part's rule holds for
them too: until the first scored meeting a row changes only through a later
harness PR that says what changed and why, and from then on nothing changes.

## Frozen choices

| Choice | What the harness does | PR |
|---|---|---|
| The advisers, deployed | Each adviser's `agents.yaml` entry is its panel agent config, with nothing under `persona` added or changed, and: the `quality` alias; temperature 0.7; no tools, so only the built-in note tools remain and channel recall is denied; the panel's permissions, relationships, reactive autonomy and 32 000-token, 200-turn conversation window. Where the panel is silent it takes the shipped reactive personas' values: 2 retries and a 300-second timeout, which nothing on the persona path reads, one action a tick, idle after five ticks, and their notes settings. A turn ends at the runtime's own 300-second event timeout, and the provider library keeps its own retries. No reply limit and no timezone is set. Each adviser keeps its memory in a file of its own | 5a |
| The deployment's config | The shipped `optimization.yaml`, with the `quality`, `fast` and `summarizer` aliases all on `claude-sonnet-4-6` at $3.00 and $15.00 per million tokens, one pricing row for that model, the global, per-workflow and per-agent spending limits at 0, which turns each off, and the arm's memory budget. The shipped `channels.yaml` fleet settings, declaring only the meeting's channel; the shipped `security.yaml` and `ui.yaml`. The written config must pass the schemas `make validate` uses before anything starts | 5a |
| The rate limiter | Off, with `SECURITY_RATE_LIMIT_ENABLED=false`. The advisers send no ID of their own, so all four share one anonymous bucket of 60 requests a minute ([ISSUE-0111](../issues/ISSUE-0111-anonymous-wallet-rpcs-share-rate-limit-bucket.md)), and a refused publish drops an adviser's reply from the channel, though its model call is in the call log; on the mock provider it dropped the chair's memo. The switch also turns off the circuit breaker and the per-agent limit on the wallet's gRPC calls. A meeting whose orchestrator has not logged the limiter off is refused before the operator speaks. The harness's own requests carry the ID `exp001-harness` | 5a |
| The meeting's channel | Declared in the deployment's `channels.yaml`, shaped like the shipped `roundtable`: the arm's members and governance from `panel.yaml`, the operator as an observer, armed, with the organisation's one-line description as its description, which every adviser's prompt shows, the topic "Advice for" and the organisation, an empty agenda, the panel's goal and convener, round limit 8, a 2 000 000-token budget, depth cap 5 and classification `internal`. It is named `advice-` and the meeting's place in its series, the same in every arm | 5a |
| A deployment per meeting | Arms B and C hold each meeting in a directory of its own, refused unless empty, which holds the deployment, the call log and the meeting's record. The processes run this checkout's orchestrator binary and `python -m agents.server`, on loopback, and inherit no `PERSATRIX_` or `SECURITY_` setting from the harness's shell. The model client's own settings, such as `ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL`, pass through, as they do to arm A's calls. The meeting's clock anchor is the moment its deployment starts. The advisers stop first, each draining its memory summaries, then the orchestrator; a process still running 90 seconds after its stop signal is killed. A hangup or `SIGTERM` stops the deployment as Ctrl-C does, and if the stop is itself interrupted, every process still running is killed | 5a |
| The close | The first close the orchestrator logs for the channel after the operator's message, by end vote or by bounded close (round limit, depth cap or cost), or the end of the first 600 seconds with no message, between two messages or since the last, whichever comes first; 600 seconds is the orchestrator's idle window, which no channel here changes. The record says what closed the discussion as part 2 reports it: a vote, the depth cap, the round limit, the cost bound or the idle window; the depth cap is the bounded close of an interaction the orchestrator logged at that cap. The memo request keeps the orchestrator's own stamp of that close in the transcript, and a stamp naming another close is recorded as a failure. Nothing else ends the wait: disarming while the chair's closing synthesis is pending would abandon the close | 5a |
| The memo turn | After a close, the harness waits until the orchestrator has no turn in flight in the channel, for at most 5 minutes, so a turn sent before the close posts while the channel is still armed. Then, at every meeting but the briefing, it disarms the channel at the configuration revision it has just read, makes the three other advisers observers, reads each adviser's energy from its store, and posts the panel's plan or recall instruction as the operator, mentioning only the chair. The memo turn's time is the harness's real time just before that post. The memo is the chair's first message after the request, leaving out a closing synthesis that ran late (marked `synthesis_reply`) | 5a |
| Failures a meeting shows | A discussion still open 60 minutes after the operator's message never closed; that is recorded, and no memo turn is held, so the meeting has no memo. A chair that has not answered 35 minutes after the request wrote no memo: a persona turn ends at the runtime's 300-second event timeout, and a turn that runs out posts nothing, so 35 minutes covers the memo turn and six turns queued ahead of it. Any other message after the request, 10 seconds after the memo, is recorded as the memo turn going on. A process that exited before the harness stopped it, and a lease refused for any reason but a spending limit, are recorded too. None of these is retried: retries come with PR 5b. A meeting that fails midway leaves a record naming the error. A lease a spending limit refused fails the meeting once it is stopped and recorded (check 6), and an orchestrator that serves no wallet fails it before the operator speaks | 5a |
| Attempts and tries | An attempt is one run of a series from its briefing: 1, or 2 once it has started again. A try is one holding of a meeting within an attempt, counted from 1. Every call-log line carries both, as the tags `attempt` and `try`, and a line without either is refused. Arms B and C hold each try in a directory of its own, `attempt-N/<meeting>/try-M` under the arm's series directory; arm A logs each series to one file | 5b |
| Provider errors | A failed call whose exception class is `RateLimitError`, `InternalServerError` (any 5xx, the 529 "overloaded" included), `APITimeoutError`, `APIConnectionError` or `TimeoutError`. The provider library retries these twice itself before one reaches the call log; those retries are not counted. One such line anywhere in a try, from any adviser and for any purpose, means the meeting is held again, since a persona turn that ends in one posts nothing. After three retries the series starts again from its briefing, once; if a meeting then fails the same way, the series is dropped from every arm's comparisons, and with fewer than four series kept the run is incomplete | 5b |
| Failures the system causes, by the call log | A call the provider refused as malformed (`BadRequestError`, `UnprocessableEntityError`) is recorded and not retried: the runtime built the request. For arm A, the meeting then has no reply | 5b |
| Harness faults | Every other exception class in the call log, `AuthenticationError`, `PermissionDeniedError` and `NotFoundError` among them, or one a later SDK adds; for arms B and C, anything a meeting raises except a start failure, which covers a rate limiter left on, a wallet not served, a spending limit that refused a lease, a config that fails its schemas, an orchestrator reply the harness cannot read and a call log it cannot read. Each stops the run with `HarnessFault`, naming the arm, series, meeting, attempt and try | 5b |
| A deployment that does not start | A process that exits while starting, or advisers not all registered within 120 seconds, before the operator's message. The meeting is held again, in a new directory, like a provider error; one that still has not started after three retries is a harness fault | 5b |
| Dollars per plan, by try | Only the calls of each meeting's try that finished count, in the attempt that finished; a try that is not known is refused rather than guessed. Every try's calls are real spend | 5b |
