## The five locks

Three the amendment named; two the surface audit forced.

- **Record shape: `(principal, speaker, scope)` — resolved, not re-opened here.** The [Phase 0 gate](issues/ISSUE-0082-residuals-phase0-gate.md) decided both axes on one live run (principal 2026-08-07, speaker 2026-08-21). Residuals PRs 3–4 own both consequences — the reserve re-size, and per-speaker-per-tenant fragmentation of close-derived group memory, a release-note obligation fixed now. **The milestone adds two**, both in the [reserve-sizing record](issues/ISSUE-0082-residuals-reserve-sizing.md): ship the clamp **signal** with the re-size rather than deferring it alongside the constant, and move the *threshold* basis with the reserve basis — [`bounded_close.go`](../internal/channels/bounded_close.go)'s one-directional requirement inverts once a per-speaker reserve exceeds the member count it is compared against, and that half is Go, not residuals PR 4.
- **ISSUE-0130(b) is not gated on R-2 — two stores, two migrations.** `principal_id` on `messages` is `sqlite_schema.go` **v11 → v12** (Go); the speaker axis is persona-memory **17 → 18** (Python). The databases are disjoint (`agents/` issues no query against `messages`) and R-2's shape is in-memory, adding no column to share. They meet at the wire, not in a schema — per the 2026-08-23 correction on ISSUE-0130.
- **ISSUE-0125 rides — in shape (4), before the live restart legs.** The amendment's cut clause stands (it cuts if the shape grows into registry persistence). Shape: **agent-side re-registration driven by the orchestrator channel's gRPC connectivity state**, triggered by **any departure from `READY` and return** — *not* the literal `TRANSIENT_FAILURE` cycle, which an idle channel never reaches, so a watcher written to it tests green and never fires. Event-driven, not a heartbeat: periodic re-register is the polling shape [RFC 0024](rfcs/0024-event-driven-scheduling.md) removed. *A deliberate deviation from the amendment's wording, on ISSUE-0125's own analysis; reversible at review.* Precondition: `Register` becomes an **upsert** — which does not itself fix the restart case. Citations on [ISSUE-0125](issues/ISSUE-0125-agents-never-reregister-after-orchestrator-restart.md).
- **Replay must be idempotent — the reconnect path is only one door.** *Forced by the plan-opening audit.* Catch-up has no watermark (RFC 0011 OQ #8) **and no dedup**, so K restarts inside the window produce `K × N` turns. Shape (a) bounded that by skipping derivation for *every* replayed span — and narrowing that skip is precisely B2's job, handing the growth back under the *correct* principal. **So B2 carries a dedup or watermark check, not just the narrowed skip**; that is an acceptance condition. The reconnect corollary rides with it (C1 must not re-run the startup tail; a test pins it) — but it guards a door C1 creates, while the restart door is open today. Reasoning on [ISSUE-0130](issues/ISSUE-0130-catchup-replay-rederives-memory-under-default-principal.md).
- **One live arc, one MT.** [MT-MEMORY-GROUP-TENANT-001](manual-tests/MT-MEMORY-GROUP-TENANT-001.md) (**ten** legs, 0–9 as of PR B2, which added Leg 9 and corrected the sign-off count) is the release gate, **extended with a restart leg** that snapshots episode and fact counts in **both** partitions: after B2 duplicates land under the *attributed* principal, so the `local`-only read ISSUE-0130 originally framed would pass while the regression is worst. Two further MT edits need owners, not closeout notes — Leg 4 widens to `(principal_id, speaker_id, summary)` (it collects pairs today, so ISSUE-0131 would have no live evidence), and [MT-MEMORY-MULTIUSER-001](manual-tests/MT-MEMORY-MULTIUSER-001.md)'s Edge Case 2 **widens** back to the now-true claim, the group MT's contrary "narrow" note being stale since v0.3.14 prep. Per [ISSUE-0126](issues/ISSUE-0126-mt-orchestrator-restart-registry-note-missing.md) option 1 **as corrected 2026-08-23**, **both** warnings are deleted, the eight unguarded restart steps confirmed safe.
