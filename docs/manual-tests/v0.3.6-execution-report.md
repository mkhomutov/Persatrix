## MT-CHANNEL-GOV-002 — Floor control: ordered, mutually-aware multi-persona replies (live, Anthropic)

Live on the Anthropic stack. The human `operator` was added to `group:planning` (`channel join`) and posted one open prompt `@`-mentioning `ember-owl`; the timeline was polled (`GET /api/v1/channels/group:planning/messages`) until the round completed.

| Step | Expected | Observed | Result |
|------|----------|----------|--------|
| 1 — channel resolves floor control ON | `group:planning` with three persona members | `group:planning` → members `ember-owl`, `iron-fox`, `nova-sparrow`; `floor_control: true` in `config/channels.yaml` (in-process state, not surfaced in the REST shape by design); boot log `channels: subsystem ready` | ✅ |
| 2 — replies land sequentially; mentioned first | one-at-a-time; `ember-owl` first | **ember-owl** (+9.3 s, mentioned-first) → **iron-fox** (+21.7 s) → **nova-sparrow** (+40.1 s); each reply landed after the previous (Δ +12.4 s, +18.4 s) — a serialized round, not a near-simultaneous burst | ✅ |
| 3 — mutual awareness; ≥1 yield | later speakers read predecessors; ≥1 declines to repeat | **iron-fox** opens "I'll weigh in while Ember Owl thinks it through" and builds on the Postgres case rather than restating blind; **nova-sparrow** is aware of both — "This one's squarely in @ember-owl's wheelhouse — **they should lead the call here**… @ember-owl's read on the infra constraints matters more than mine" — and **yields**, adding only the product angle instead of re-arguing infra | ✅ |
| 4 — REST order + non-overlapping timing | personas ordered, monotonic timestamps | history (newest-first) shows one human message + three persona messages; persona timestamps strictly monotonic `ember-owl < iron-fox < nova-sparrow` (`04:40:04` → `04:40:16` → `04:40:35` UTC), non-overlapping | ✅ |
| 5 — per-turn timeout (optional) | a silent responder times out at ~45 s without stalling | **not run** (all three responders replied; the deterministic timeout-advance is pinned by `fanout_floor_test.go::TestFloorRound_TimeoutAdvances`, green above) | — (optional) |

**Contrast with the pre-amendment baseline.** Before floor control all three would have answered the *original prompt only*, in parallel, mutually blind — three independent Postgres-vs-SQLite essays with no cross-reference. Here iron-fox **references** ember-owl and nova-sparrow **defers to** ember-owl and **yields** on the infra call: floor control is unambiguously engaged.

**MT-CHANNEL-GOV-002: ✅ Pass (live).** Ordered, mutually-aware, serialized round with a clear yield — the headline "open a URL and watch a coherent conversation" claim, demonstrated live on Anthropic.

**Recipe note (folded in).** The recipe's Step 2 `channel send … --as operator` returns `403 Forbidden: sender is not a member of the channel` unless the human first joins (`persatrix channel join planning --as operator`); the recipe is updated to add the join precondition. The CLI binary is `bin/persatrix` (orchestrator binary is `bin/persatrix-server`; the MT's `./bin/orchestrator` reference is corrected).
