| 1 | Monorepo, personas NAMED | 99 s | cascade cap (depth 5) → structural, chair synthesis | 7 msgs; 47,474 tok / $0.167 incl. close path |

| 3 (retro) | Broad/bland, unnamed | 41 s | **end-vote quorum** (threshold 2) | No stall — a warmed store engaged a bland opener; no chair synthesis (end-vote close arms none, by design) |
| 4 (housekeeping, post-F-3) | Settle-prone, 3-item agenda | 2m41s | cascade → structural | 2 cadence advances; the F-3 duplicate-vote shape re-fanned and terminated |
| 5–6 (standing standups ×2) | Unchanged topic per fire | ~22 s each | **end-vote quorum** ×2 | Pure record-confirmations — see finding 3 |
| 7 (four-vendor, `mode: off`) | Multi-cloud, personas named | 67 s | cascade → structural | 117,757 tok / $0.25 across 4 vendors; watsonx seat authored 13 of the turns (llama chattiest by far — a per-seat cadence-skew datapoint) |

Calibration findings (feeding [ISSUE-0109](../issues/ISSUE-0109-rfc0052-autonomous-defaults-calibration.md)):

1. **`max_cascade_depth` (5) binds before `max_rounds` on every productive arc** — ≈4–5 hops per discussion regardless of `max_rounds=6/8/12`, which never fired live. The cascade cap is the *de facto* length knob.
2. **Collapse-proneness inversely tracks accumulated channel memory**: a fresh store muted bare openers (2026-07-20); this warmed store engaged even a bland retro. Naming personas in the topic remains the fresh-store lever.
3. **Re-convening an unchanged topic yields push-back and a redundant synthesis** (arc 2) or ~20 s confirmation stubs (arcs 5–6) — standing channels need per-convening topic freshness.
4. **`end_vote_threshold=2` closes a 3-persona channel fast** (4 of 7 arcs closed on votes) — co-tune with `max_rounds` for longer exploration; the bounded close is the net, not the norm.
5. **Reserve headroom**: every close path fit inside the `1 + N` reserve; zero close-path denials post-F-1.
